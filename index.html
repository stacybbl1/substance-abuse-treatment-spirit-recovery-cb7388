About

Who we are

Welcome to New Spirit Recovery, a substance abuse treatment center in Encino, California dedicated to helping individuals break free from addiction and rebuild their lives with lasting purpose. Serving the greater Los Angeles area, New Spirit Recovery offers a full continuum of evidence-based care designed to meet clients wherever they are in their recovery journey. With a compassionate, clinically experienced team and individualized treatment planning at the core of every program, the facility creates a supportive environment where healing is not just possible but sustainable. From medically supervised detox through long-term aftercare planning, New Spirit Recovery provides the structure and expertise that recovery demands.

Programs

What we do

Substance Abuse Treatment

Comprehensive, individualized care for alcohol and drug addiction using evidence-based clinical methods.

Partial Hospitalization Program (PHP)

Structured full-day treatment providing intensive therapy and medical support without overnight residential stay.

Intensive Outpatient Program (IOP)

Flexible, high-level outpatient programming that allows clients to maintain daily responsibilities while receiving consistent clinical care.

Dual Diagnosis Treatment

Integrated care that addresses co-occurring mental health disorders alongside substance use disorders simultaneously.

Individual and Group Therapy

Licensed therapists deliver one-on-one counseling and facilitated group sessions targeting the root causes of addiction.

Aftercare and Relapse Prevention Planning

Personalized continuing care plans and alumni support designed to sustain recovery long after formal treatment ends.
